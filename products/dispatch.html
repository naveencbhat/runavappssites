Dispatch

Scheduling & dispatch for small transport operators
beta

About

A dispatcher board assigns trips to vehicles and drivers with live conflict detection (double bookings, capacity, wheelchair lifts, expired licenses or insurance — all overridable with an audit trail). Riders request rides from their own app, drivers follow a one-tap manifest, and billing exports per-payer CSV logs for Medicaid brokers. Email and SMS reminders ride on SMTP + Twilio/Telnyx.
