K12 Sphere

Learning platform for Kindergarten to Grade 12
beta

About

One shared UI in packages/ui runs on iOS, Android and web. Students get grade-specific tasks, spaced-repetition reviews, a mistakes notebook, badges, weekly goals, read-aloud support and a daily 5-minute mix. Admins build tasks or generate them from the built-in US curriculum library stored in MongoDB.

Highlights

  • 363 curriculum-aligned tasks across 4 subjects
  • 12 interactive question types incl. read-aloud scoring and number lines
  • Spaced repetition: reviews after 3, 7, 21 and 60 days
  • Badges, streaks, weekly goals and teacher feedback
