About

Bharatham combines a Node/Express + Prisma + PostgreSQL API, a Next.js admin portal and an Expo mobile/web app so contributors can add entries, reviewers approve them, and readers explore India's heritage in English, Hindi and Kannada. The seed corpus ships with 219 entries, 96 references and quizzes, and deployment is a single Vercel deployment serving the API, admin and public web app.
